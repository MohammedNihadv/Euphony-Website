The Philosophy

Why Euphony Exists

Somewhere along the way, music players became subscription services, shuffle became a premium feature, and ads started interrupting your playlists. Euphony is a music player that simply gets out of your way.

💸

Subscription Music Apps

Modern proprietary services

  • ✕ Monthly subscription fees required just to play music without ads
  • ✕ Basic features like Shuffle locked behind a premium paywall
  • ✕ Constant upsell banners interrupting your favorite albums
  • ✕ Mandatory cloud accounts and invasive telemetry tracking
  • ✕ Your offline library can be removed by licensing disputes

Euphony Open Source

Crafted For Performance

Everything You Need, Nothing You Don't

Built with Flutter, Riverpod, Drift SQLite, and Just Audio for lightning-fast playback across platforms.

We don't track your listening habits. No analytics scripts, no phone-home telemetry, no third-party SDKs.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Euphony, our Neo-Brutalist design, and open-source license.

Why is Euphony 100% free with no ads or subscriptions? +

Because we believe software should respect its users. Somewhere along the way, playing your own music became a subscription service where basic features like shuffle are locked behind paywalls. Euphony was built as a radical alternative: open source, ad-free, and forever free.

How do offline downloads and local libraries work? +

Euphony uses high-performance Drift SQLite for local database caching and `just_audio` for playback. You can easily import your existing offline music collection or listen to your cached library without an internet connection.

What is Neo-Brutalist design? +

Neo-Brutalism is a modern design philosophy featuring bold typography, high-contrast borders (`2.5px solid #121218`), hard offset shadows (`4px 4px 0px`), and intentional color accents. Unlike generic glassmorphic UI, every element in Euphony is tactile and clear.

How can I contribute or report an issue? +

Whether you want to fix a typo, add a new theme, or optimize performance — contributions are always welcome! Visit our official GitHub repository to fork the codebase, open an issue, or submit a Pull Request.
