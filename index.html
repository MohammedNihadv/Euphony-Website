The Core Philosophy

Crafted for Listeners, Not Advertisers

Most music platforms treat your listening habits as product data and lock basic player controls behind paywalls. Euphony was built from the ground up to respect you.

True Offline Ownership

Your music belongs to you. Store lossless audio locally on your device without fearing sudden catalog pull-downs, expired streaming licenses, or spotty network dropouts.

Permanent Library

Zero Advertisements

No audio ads between songs, no sponsored popups, and no artificial restrictions. Essential playback tools like shuffle, repeat, and sleep timer are always 100% unlocked.

Uninterrupted Flow

Absolute Privacy

No telemetry, no background analytics, no mandatory accounts, and no tracking beacons. What you listen to remains entirely private on your device under GPL v3.0.

Zero Telemetry
Crafted For Performance

Everything You Need, Nothing You Don't

Built with Flutter, Riverpod, Drift SQLite, and Just Audio for lightning-fast playback across platforms.

Material 3 & Neo-Brutalist UI

Beautiful Material 3 experience with bold typography, crisp hard shadows (`4px 4px 0px`), and an intentional 3-accent palette designed for WCAG AA readability.

Offline & Local Library

Full support for local music files, offline downloads, smart audio caching, and persistent playback state powered by Drift SQLite.

3-Way Adaptive Theme

Switch seamlessly between warm Light Paper, deep Slate Dark, and true OLED/AMOLED Pure Black themes.

Fast Search & Queue

Fast search across tracks, albums, artists & playlists with full queue management, shuffle, and repeat without UI lag.

Background Playback

Background playback with native media notification controls, lock screen artwork, and playback speed controls with sleep timer.

Privacy & Zero Telemetry

We don't track your listening habits. No analytics scripts, no phone-home telemetry, no third-party SDKs.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Euphony, our Neo-Brutalist design, and open-source license.

What is Euphony Music, and how is it different? +

Euphony Music is a modern, 100% ad-free, open-source music player application engineered with Flutter and SQLite. Designed for Android, Windows, macOS, and Linux, it gives you full control over your local and offline audio library with zero telemetry, zero subscriptions, and an expressive Neo-Brutalist design.

Is Euphony Music related to the Discord bot or the Indian band? +

No. Euphony Music is an independent open-source cross-platform music player application created by Mohammed Nihad V. It is completely unrelated to the Discord music bot or the Mumbai musical ensemble Euphony Official.

Why is Euphony Music 100% free with no ads or subscriptions? +

Because we believe software should respect its users. Playing your music shouldn't require a monthly paywall or suffer through unskippable ads. Euphony Music was built as a radical, GPL-3.0 licensed community alternative: free forever.

How do offline downloads and local libraries work? +

Euphony Music uses high-performance Drift SQLite for local database caching and just_audio for background audio playback. You can easily import your existing offline music collection (MP3, FLAC, WAV, AAC, OPUS) and listen anywhere with zero internet connection needed.

What is Neo-Brutalist design? +

Neo-Brutalism is a bold design movement featuring high-contrast borders (2.5px solid #121218), tactile offset drop-shadows (4px 4px 0px), vibrant accent hues, and clean typography. It rejects bland generic minimalism in favor of a playful, tactile interface.

Where can I download the Euphony Music APK and installers? +

Official binaries are available directly on our Downloads Section and via our GitHub Releases page with SHA-256 integrity checksums for Android (APK), Windows (Setup EXE), macOS (DMG), and Linux (.deb package & AppImage).
